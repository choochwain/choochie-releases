How points work

Every bit of energy she uses - in the app on any PC signed in to your account, or in web chat - is 1 point, and energy you buy adds a 0.25-point bonus per energy when it lands. Points count from when the leaderboard launched. "This month" resets on the 1st (UTC); your level is based on all-time points.
