What is energy?

Everything she thinks, says and sees - in the app and in the web chat - uses a little energy; voice and looking at your screen use more than a text reply. When she's out she still walks around and uses her own lines - she just can't hold a conversation.

How do I unlock a plan?

Patreon is coming soon. For now you buy a plan on her Discord: open a ticket, pay directly, and you get a code. Redeem it on your account page or in the app (Settings > Energy > Redeem code). Your plan follows you to every PC you sign in on, for as long as you paid for. Refund requests: e-mail support@choochie.app - handled case by case. Terms

Buy a plan on Discord

  1. Join her Discord and open the buy channel.

  3. Pay the way the ticket says, then post your confirmation there.
  4. You get a code in the ticket and in your DMs. Redeem it on your account page or in the app.
