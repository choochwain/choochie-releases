1. Summary

Choochie is an AI desktop companion app for Windows with an optional web chat on this site. Sign-in (Discord, Patreon, X, Google) is only used to identify your account and sync energy/perks; it is never used to generate images. We never request or store your e-mail address or password, we don't keep provider access tokens after you sign in, and we don't store your chat messages, pictures or voice recordings. Her memory of you lives on your own PC. We don't sell data, run ads or use analytics trackers.

2. Who we are and how to contact us

The data controller for Choochie (the Windows app, its server and this website) is the independent developer who publishes Choochie at choochwain.github.io/choochie-releases.

E-mail: support.choochiespet@gmail.com (privacy questions, data access and deletion requests - we answer within 30 days).

Also: open a ticket in our Discord server (invite on our links page) or message us on Patreon. Please say "privacy request" so we can find it quickly. We answer privacy requests within 30 days.

We cannot post, send messages or act on your behalf on any of these services.

4. What your account stores

Our server stores, in its database (Upstash Redis):

  • Account record: a random account id, your account name, when the account was created, the user id of each linked sign-in provider, the device ids of up to 10 PCs signed in to the account, and the amount of starter energy granted.
  • Login index: provider + provider user id → account id, so we can find your account when you sign in.
  • Energy and Orbs: extra energy on the account (starter energy plus anything bought), and your web-chat Orb balance with the date of its last daily refill.
  • Patreon pledge: for Patreon logins, your tier level and until when it runs (also updated by Patreon's membership notifications).
  • PC links: which PCs are signed in, and - if you sign in with Patreon from the app - a link from that PC to your Patreon id.
  • Anti-abuse: one-way hashes (SHA-256, shortened) of PCs that already received a starter energy grant, so starter grants can't be farmed.
  • Roadmap: your votes per month and any ideas you post (linked to your account id).

Why: to run your account and give you the energy, Orbs and perks you're entitled to (performance of our agreement with you), and to prevent abuse of free grants (legitimate interest).

5. Web chat on this website

  • What you send: your message (max 800 characters), up to 16 earlier turns that your browser sends back for context (text only), and optionally one picture (JPEG/PNG/WebP, max 4 MB, downscaled in your browser first) and/or one voice message (max 60 seconds).
  • What happens to it: text goes to the chat AI model; a picture goes to the vision AI model as part of that single request; a voice message goes to speech-to-text and the transcript comes back to you; if you turn on "speak replies", her reply text goes to text-to-speech and the audio comes back to you.
  • Nothing is stored. Messages, pictures, audio, transcripts and replies are not written to our database, disk or logs. Pictures and audio exist only in server memory while your request is answered. The server keeps no chat history.
  • In your browser: the last 50 messages are kept in this tab's session storage so the page survives a reload; they are deleted when you close the tab, or when you press "Clear chat".
  • Camera and microphone are only used after you press the snapshot or hold-to-talk button and your browser asks for permission. A snapshot is shown to you first and only sent when you press send.
  • Stored side effects: your Orb balance; anonymous daily counters (number of requests and AI tokens, no ids; kept 120 days); and, in memory only, rate-limit timestamps per account and IP address for the last 60 seconds.

6. The desktop app

Stays on your PC

Her memory of you (facts, a conversation summary, recent turns), her diary, your settings and your chat history live in %APPDATA%\ClinglyPet on your computer. Delete that folder, or uninstall and delete it, and it's gone.

Sent to the server (only what's needed for a reply)

  • Device id: a random id created on first run, sent with each request to count energy and link your plan and account. Statistics only use a one-way hash of it.
  • Chat: what you type or say, plus her instructions, which include the parts of her local memory relevant to the reply. The server forwards this to the AI provider and does not log or store it.
  • Microphone (only when you turn it on): voice detection runs on your PC; only finished sentences are sent for speech-to-text, never silence or a continuous stream. Audio is not stored; only the text comes back.
  • Screenshots and webcam (only when you trigger them): "look at my screen" (after a separate consent, captures only the front window), the optional supporter "peek" feature (off by default, needs screen consent), "watch & play" (frames of the game window while it's on), a photo of her on your desktop, a webcam snapshot when you ask her to look at you, and photos you choose to send. Windows that look private (password managers, sign-in, checkout, banking, medical, incognito) are never captured, nor chat and mail apps unless you allow them, nor anything on your "never watch" list. Images go to the vision model for that one reply and are not stored.
  • Her voice: her reply text goes to text-to-speech.
  • Discord DMs (optional perk): we store your device id → Discord user id, username and link date until you unlink. DMs you send her wait in server memory (max 10) until the app fetches them.
  • Discord server presence (when a server admin invites her): the last 100 lines of that channel's conversation with her (author id, display name, text, time) are kept until she is removed from the channel. Messages that don't involve her aren't stored.
  • Desktop "pranks" (cursor tricks, minimising windows, wallpaper, the "virus" and ChoochieOS effects) run locally, are off by default and need your consent; they don't send anything to us.

Why: to provide the features you use (agreement with you) and, for camera, microphone and screen features, your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by switching the feature off.

8. Google user data

If you choose "Continue with Google", we request only the openid and profile scopes. We use your Google account identifier to recognise your Choochie account on future sign-ins, and your given name as your display name in Choochie. We do not request your e-mail address, contacts, Drive, Gmail, photos or any other Google data, and we don't keep your Google access token after sign-in.

Choochie's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, data received from Google is used only to provide sign-in to Choochie; it is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train or improve AI models, not used to generate images, and not shared with anyone except as needed to run the service (our hosting and database providers) or when required by law. No human reads it except when you ask us for help with your account or when required for security or by law. You can remove the Google login from your account page at any time, and revoke Choochie's access in your Google Account settings.

9. This website

  • No cookies, no ads, no analytics.
  • Local storage in your browser: your session token (if signed in), your account name for the header, that you confirmed you're 18+, and small preferences (such as "speak replies" and the 18+ chat toggle). Signing out or clearing site data removes them.
  • Session storage: the last 50 web-chat messages, only in the current tab.
  • Third-party requests: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the download pages ask the GitHub API for the latest version; both receive your IP address and browser details as part of the request.

10. Content policy

Choochie is a text and voice companion. She does not generate or edit images. The following is never allowed, in the app, the web chat or her Discord presence, and her instructions refuse it automatically:

  • Any sexual content involving minors, or characters presented as minors.
  • Creating, describing, editing or sexualising intimate imagery of real people, including photos users upload. Non-consensual intimate imagery and "deepfakes" are prohibited.
  • Uploading or showing her images of other people without their consent. Only send pictures of yourself, or of people who agreed.

Anything with a picture attached is always answered in non-explicit mode. The optional 18+ mode is for adult supporters (Pet plan and up) who switch it on themselves, and it is text only. Accounts that break these rules are closed. See the Terms of Service.

11. How long we keep data

  • Chat messages, pictures, audio, transcripts: not stored (memory only, for the duration of the request).
  • Account record, logins, energy, Orbs, PC links: until you delete the account, unlink that login or sign that PC out.
  • Monthly roadmap votes: expire after 400 days.
  • Anonymous daily statistics: 120 days.
  • Rate-limit data: memory only, 60 seconds; starter-orb IP checks are cleared daily.
  • Discord DM link / server channel memory: until you unlink, or until she is removed from the channel.
  • Starter-energy hashes (one-way, anti-abuse; see section 12): kept until you ask us to remove them.

12. How to delete your data

Delete your account yourself: sign in, open your account page and press Delete account. This immediately removes your account record, every login link, the PCs linked to the account, your extra energy (including unspent bought energy), your Orb balance and this month's roadmap votes. Ideas you posted stay on the roadmap without your name or account link. Your session stops working at once.

Everything goes. The button also removes what is tied to your linked PCs and your Patreon login: each PC's plan, tester and Patreon links, energy bought on those PCs, their Ko-fi pay codes and Discord links, your Patreon pledge record, and your roadmap votes from every month. Suggestions you posted on the roadmap stay but lose any link to you. The only thing kept is a one-way hash of each PC that already received free starter energy (it cannot be turned back into a device id and stops free-energy farming). To remove even that, e-mail support.choochiespet@gmail.com.

On your PC: uninstall Choochie (Settings > Apps) and delete %APPDATA%\ClinglyPet. Discord DMs: unlink in the app. Provider access: you can also revoke Choochie in your Discord, Patreon, X or Google account settings.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or California law), you have the right to access the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing). To use these rights, contact us. If you're in the EU/EEA or UK you can also complain to your local data protection authority. We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

14. Children

Choochie is for adults only: you must be 18 or older to use the app, the website and the web chat, and the 18+ mode additionally requires an adult supporter account and your own opt-in. The service is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13, or under 16 in the EU/EEA. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete the account and its data.

15. Security

All traffic to our server and this website is encrypted (HTTPS). Sessions are signed tokens; we keep no passwords and no provider tokens. Access to the database and server is limited to the developer.

16. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when Choochie's data practices change, and change the "last updated" date above. For significant changes we'll also post a notice in the app's update notes.
