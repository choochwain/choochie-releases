Privacy Policy

What Choochie collects, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

1. Summary

Choochie is an AI desktop companion app for Windows, with an optional web chat on this site. Sign-in only identifies your account and syncs your energy and perks. We never ask for your e-mail address or password, we don't keep sign-in providers' access tokens, and we don't store your chat messages, pictures or voice messages. In the app, her memory of you lives on your own PC; for the web chat we keep only a short text memory of you that you can delete any time. We don't sell your personal data, and there are no ads or tracking on this site or in the app.

2. Who we are and how to contact us

Choochie (the Windows app, its server, the web chat and this website) is run by the developer of Choochie ("we"), who is responsible for the data described here.

Contact: support.choochiespet@gmail.com, or our Discord server. For privacy questions or to get a copy of, correct or delete your data, write "privacy request" and we'll answer within one month.

3. Sign-in providers

You can use the desktop pet without an account. For the AI features and the web chat you sign in with Discord, X, Patreon (coming soon) or - if it is offered on the login page - Google. We ask for the smallest permission that identifies you. The provider's access token is used once while you sign in and is not stored. We can't post, send messages or act for you on these services.

4. What we collect and why

  • Your account - to run it and give you what you're entitled to: an account id, your account name, your linked logins, the PCs signed in to it (up to 10), your energy balance, your Patreon tier or redeemed plan code (which plan and until when), and your roadmap votes and ideas. If you join the leaderboard, your chosen nickname, level and points are shown publicly; leave any time.
  • Web chat - to answer you: your message, the recent conversation (sent by your browser for context) and, only if you choose, a picture, webcam snapshot or voice message. These are passed to our AI providers to create her reply and are not stored by us. Your browser keeps the conversation in this tab only, until you close it. On a video call, small snapshots of your camera are sent the same way and never stored; she keeps only a few short text notes of what she saw (for example "holding a mug, blue hoodie") in our server's working memory so she doesn't repeat herself. They are not written to our database and are gone 10 minutes after the call.
  • Her memory of you (signed-in accounts) - so she remembers you next time, we keep a short text summary and a few facts from your web chats (for example your nickname, things you like, topics you talked about, or a few words about what you showed her on a call). It never contains pictures, video or audio, and we try to keep sensitive details out of it. You can delete it any time with Forget me in the chat menu; it is also deleted with your account, or after 180 days without chatting.
  • Play progress (signed-in accounts) - for the levels, streaks and games on this site: your bond level and XP, the days you visited (the last 60), your kisses, affection meter, badges, outfits unlocked and worn, today's quest progress and game stats (for example wins and best scores). It is kept with your account. If you join the leaderboard, your bond level, title and streak are shown next to your nickname.
  • Desktop app - your settings, her memories and your chat history stay on your PC. To create a reply, the app sends your message and, only when you turn them on or ask for them, screenshots, photos, webcam snapshots or your voice (only while you speak). Optional "notice what I'm doing" features read the active window's title and app name on your PC; only the type of app is sent, never titles or content, and password managers, banking and private windows are skipped.
  • Payments - you pay on Ko-fi, Streamlabs, Patreon (coming soon) or, for plans bought through a Discord ticket, with the payment method shown in your ticket; we never see your card details. We receive what we need to give you what you paid for (your Patreon tier, a Ko-fi payment confirmation, or the payment confirmation you post in your ticket). A Ko-fi tip sent without your code waits under one-way hashes of its transaction ID and the e-mail used, until you claim it with both.
  • Plan codes and tickets - we store only a one-way hash of each plan code, which plan it unlocks and for how long, and when it was used (not the code itself). A purchase ticket on Discord is a private channel between you and staff; it is deleted when the ticket closes, and a text copy may be kept for our records (codes removed).
  • Security - to stop abuse (for example repeated free starter energy), we use short-term checks and keep a one-way fingerprint of PCs that already received starter energy. Our hosting provider keeps technical logs, which never contain your messages, pictures or audio.

6. Google user data

If Google sign-in is offered and you choose "Continue with Google", we request only the openid and profile permissions. We use your Google account identifier to recognise your Choochie account when you sign in again, and your first name as your display name. We store only these two items, with your account, until you remove the Google login or delete your account. We don't request your e-mail address, contacts, Drive, Gmail, photos or any other Google data, and we don't keep your Google access token.

Choochie's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data from Google is used only to provide sign-in to Choochie. It is never used to generate images, not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI models, and not shared except with our hosting and database providers to run the service, or where the law requires it. You can remove the Google login on your account page and revoke access in your Google Account settings. Choochie is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

7. This website

No cookies, ads, analytics or tracking. Your browser only stores what's needed to keep you signed in (for up to 60 days, or until you log out), remember your 18+ confirmation and chat settings, and keep your web chat in the current tab. Fonts and images are served from this site; the download and updates pages ask GitHub for the latest version.

8. How long we keep it

  • Chat messages, pictures, audio, video-call snapshots: not stored - used only to create the reply.
  • Her text memory of you (web chat): until you use Forget me, delete your account, or 180 days without chatting.
  • Play progress: until you delete your account.
  • Account data: until you delete your account (roadmap vote rounds expire after about 400 days).
  • Short-term anti-abuse checks: no more than a day. Technical logs: a limited period, under our hosting provider's policy.
  • Starter-energy fingerprint: until you ask us to remove it.

9. How to delete your data

On the website: sign in, open your account page and press Delete account. It's immediate and removes your account, all its logins, your energy, your linked PCs' plan, tester and Patreon links, energy bought on those PCs, their payment codes and Discord links, your Patreon membership record, all your roadmap votes, your play progress and leaderboard entry, and her web chat memory of you. Roadmap ideas you posted stay without your name. We keep only the one-way starter-energy fingerprint (so free energy can't be farmed); e-mail us to remove that too.

Deletion is from our live database; we don't keep our own backups, and any backups held by our database provider follow its own retention.

You can ask to see a copy of your data or have it deleted by e-mailing support.choochiespet@gmail.com. On your PC: uninstall Choochie and delete its data folder (%APPDATA%ClinglyPet). Cancel a Patreon membership on Patreon.

10. Adults only

Choochie (the app, this website and the web chat) is only for people aged 18 or over. If you believe someone under 18 has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes

We'll update this page and its date when our data practices change, and announce significant changes in the app's update notes.
